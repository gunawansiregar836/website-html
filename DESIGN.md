## Brand & Style

This design system is engineered for B2B industrial procurement, supply chain infrastructure, and heavy logistics operations. Designed specifically for industrial manufacturers, warehousing directors, and plant managers seeking reliable packaging and pallet assets, the visual language balances structural solidity with transactional efficiency.

### Personality & Tone
- **Authoritative & Industrial:** Rooted in engineering reliability, logistics rigor, and verified industrial safety standards.
- **Utilitarian & Transparent:** Clear specification sheets, prominent load-bearing metrics, transparent volume pricing, and friction-free Request-For-Quotation (RFQ) pathways.
- **High-Velocity Operational Clarity:** Instant differentiation between industrial pallet types (ISPM-15 export-compliant wooden pallets, hygienic heavy-duty HDPE plastic, custom-built cradles, and recycled waste solutions).

### Design Movement
**Modern Industrial Utility:** A convergence of high-clarity enterprise SaaS precision and rugged physical-world logistics branding. It combines structured data cards, high-contrast industrial safety cues, refined micro-borders, tactile elevation, and prominent, conversion-centric transactional actions.

## Colors

The palette establishes heavy-duty reliability through corporate industrial maritime navy hues, contrasted against safety industrial amber accents that command immediate action in RFQ pipelines.

### Color Roles & Meaning
- **Primary Navy (`#004B87` / Deep Base `#0B2545`):** The foundation of trust, institutional authority, and structured navigation. Anchors primary banners, header framing, navigation menus, and core data containers.
- **Secondary Industrial Safety Amber (`#E85D04` / Hover `#F77F00`):** Inspired by warehouse hazard stripes and safety equipment. Dedicated exclusively to top-funnel conversion CTAs, urgent RFQ triggers, price highlights, and limited stock alerts.
- **Tertiary Logistics Green (`#107E44`):** Represents verified direct B2B communication channels (WhatsApp direct sales desks, ISPM-15 environmental clearance stamps, sustainability, and recycled scrap pallet intake).
- **Neutral Industrial Slate (`#1E293B`):** Deep charcoal-slate utilized for dense technical text, structural card borders (`#E2E8F0`), high-density data tables, and subtle background alternating rows (`#F8FAFC`).

## Typography

The typography pairs **Plus Jakarta Sans** for commanding, high-impact industrial headlines with **Inter** for dense, ultra-legible technical specifications, dimension listings, RFQ calculators, and tabular invoices.

### Hierarchical Usage
- **Display & Section Headers (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Heavy geometric cuts (`700`, `800`) bring presence to manufacturing claims, product lines, and warehouse stats.
- **Specifications & Body Text (`Inter`):** Clean neutral metrics maintain pin-sharp legibility even when rendering complex pallet dimensions (`120 x 100 x 15 cm`), dynamic load capacities (Dynamic: 1,500kg / Static: 4,000kg), and RFQ terms.
- **Labels & Micro-data:** Uppercase or high-contrast semibold Inter styling for industrial badges (`ISPM 15 CERTIFIED`, `RECYCLED GRADE A`, `MINIMUM ORDER 50 PCS`).

## Layout & Spacing

A structured 12-column responsive grid engineered for product catalogs, side-by-side RFQ calculation utilities, and multi-tier specification sheets.

### Layout Principles
- **Grid Architecture:** Desktop targets a standard 12-column structure with `1.5rem` gutters and a maximum container boundary of `1280px`. Breakpoints step down gracefully: 8 columns on tablet devices (`768px - 1024px`) and 4 columns on mobile (`< 768px`).
- **Dense Data Accommodation:** Cards and product listings optimize content density using `space-sm` (`8px`) and `space-md` (`16px`) spacing internally, reserving `space-xl` (`40px`) strictly for distinct thematic divisions (e.g., transition from pallet catalog to bulk scrap buyback section).

## Elevation & Depth

To express industrial stability and physical presence, depth is communicated through crisp structural boundaries paired with calibrated, neutral shadows.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Flat/Subsurface):** `#F8FAFC` slate canvas tinting and `#F1F5F9` utility input backgrounds with no elevation.
- **Level 1 (Structural Card):** Pure white `#FFFFFF` surface framed with a 1px border (`#E2E8F0`) and subtle ambient shadow (`box-shadow: 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`).
- **Level 2 (Active/Hover/Floating Filters):** Enhanced boundary with slight primary tint (`#004B87` at 15%) and elevated directional depth (`box-shadow: 0 8px 20px -4px rgba(11, 37, 69, 0.12)`).
- **Level 3 (Modal / Quick Quote Flyout):** High-prominence enterprise modal elevation (`box-shadow: 0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.25), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.1)`), keeping critical transactional dialogs pinned above complex background operations.

## Shapes

The design system employs **Soft** geometric rounding (`roundedness: 1`), utilizing controlled 4px to 8px radiuses.

### Geometry Guidelines
- **Form Controls & Inputs:** 6px radius (`0.375rem`) providing a robust, professional look without bubbly softness.
- **Product & Spec Cards:** 8px radius (`0.5rem`) outer boundaries with matching inner media masks.
- **Status Badges & Category Tags:** 4px radius (`0.25rem`) to maintain industrial compliance tag aesthetics.
- **Primary CTA Buttons:** 6px radius (`0.375rem`) to emphasize structural solidity and deliberate action.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Industrial CTA (RFQ / Instant Quotation):** `#004B87` or `#E85D04` solid fill, white bold text, 6px radius, `0.75rem 1.5rem` padding. High visual priority; on hover, shifts to `#F77F00` or `#003866` with a crisp 1px offset ring.
- **Direct WhatsApp Procurement Button:** `#107E44` solid fill with logistics phone icon, white semibold text.
- **Secondary / Spec Sheet Download:** Transparent background, 1.5px solid `#004B87`, primary blue text; hover fills with `#F0F7FF`.

### Industrial Product Cards
- **Construction:** Pure white background, 1px `#E2E8F0` border, 8px radius.
- **Header:** High-resolution product visual framed in a neutral 4:3 container, overlaid with top-left category badge (`Pallet Kayu`, `Pallet Plastik HDPE`) and top-right stock condition badge (`Baru`, `Grade A Bekas`).
- **Body:** Product title in `headline-sm`, subtitle with standard dimensions (`1200 x 1000 x 150 mm`), static and dynamic load capacity badges, unit pricing in bold amber `#E85D04` (`Rp 150.000 / pcs`).
- **Footer:** Dual split-action bar containing "Minta Penawaran" (RFQ) and direct messaging ("Chat Sales").

### Chips & Specification Badges
- **Export Certified:** Soft green tint (`#DCFCE7`), text `#15803D`, 1px border `#BBF7D0`.
- **Heavy Duty / Hazard Alert:** Soft amber tint (`#FFEDD5`), text `#C2410C`, 1px border `#FED7AA`.
- **Dimensions / Technical Attributes:** Neutral tint (`#F1F5F9`), text `#475569`, 1px border `#CBD5E1`.

### Forms & RFQ Pallet Calculator
- **Inputs:** Crisp white fill, 1px border `#CBD5E1`, text `#0F172A`, placeholder `#94A3B8`. Focus state applies a 2px outer glow in `#004B87` with zero blur offset.
- **Select / Dropdowns:** Industrial Chevron indicator with distinct active item highlighting.
- **File Upload Area:** Dashed 2px `#94A3B8` border over `#F8FAFC` surface for spec drawing and layout diagram uploads.

### Enterprise Trust Bar & Testimonials
- Industrial client logo showcase (automotive, FMCG, logistics hubs) rendered in monochrome slate (`#64748B`) with full-color transition on interactive hover.
